How it works

1. Say the job

"Get three mover quotes for Saturday." "Find a storage unit near me." "Book the cheapest plumber."

2. It does the legwork

It finds real nearby businesses with phone numbers, and free AI writes your call script, questions and a ready text.

3. You tap to call

Call and text buttons open your phone with the number and message filled in. Log each quote as you go.

4. Book and report

Pick the winner and add it to your calendar (Google or any calendar app). You get a job report to copy or save.

Built for people who live on the phone
